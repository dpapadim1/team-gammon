Cookie policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Team Gammon sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in, and stores two small things in your browser. It uses no advertising or tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner to accept.

1. The cookie

NamePurposeLifetime
tg.sidIdentifies your sign-in session, for accounts and for guests. Strictly necessary: without it you cannot play.7 days after your last visit; removed when you sign out

It is readable only by the server (HttpOnly), is not sent to other websites (SameSite), and is marked Secure on HTTPS. It holds a random id, not your name or password.

2. Browser storage

The key tg.prefs in your browser’s local storage remembers whether “Confirm each turn” is on. The key tg.puzzles remembers your puzzle rating and how you did on each puzzle. Neither leaves your device, and both stay until you clear your browser data.

3. What we do not use

No analytics, advertising, social media or other third-party cookies, and no third-party scripts. If that changes we will update this page first and ask for consent where the law requires it.

4. Managing cookies

Your browser lets you view and delete cookies and local storage. If you block tg.sid you will not be able to sign in or play as a guest.
