How ratings work

  • Everybody starts at 1200. A team is as strong as the average of its two players.
  • Bots have fixed ratings, shown next to each level when you choose one, so a win against a stronger bot is worth more.
  • Ratings move faster during a player’s first 20 rated games; those players are shown in grey with a question mark (provisional).
  • Guests are not rated. Games with fewer than 8 turns, abandoned games and games that ended with a server error do not count.
  • Your rating, rated games and wins are public here, with your name. Deleting your account removes them.
